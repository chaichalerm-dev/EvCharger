# Business Assumptions

The demo assumes demand, favorable competition, accessibility, POI coverage, infrastructure readiness, flood safety, usable area, and business potential contribute to expansion attractiveness.

Area bands and charger ranges are planning examples. Scores are relative indicators, not feasibility certificates. Competitor factor means opportunity after considering competition, not raw competitor count. Flood safety uses an inverse quality score.

Before real use, owners must approve factor definitions, weights, thresholds, station footprints, CAPEX/OPEX, utilization, tariffs, lease/franchise economics, tax, financing, data confidence rules, and critical overrides.
