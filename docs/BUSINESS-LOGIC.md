# Business Logic

Station selection compares available area with configurable minimum footprints. Charging Point starts at 80 square metres, EV Hub at 350, and Full EV Station at 900. These are demo assumptions, not engineering standards.

Recommendations combine score, area, and critical risks. Reasons, risks, and missing information are first-class outputs. Unknown electrical capacity never becomes a positive fact.

Financial simulation uses fixed per-station investment, revenue, and operating assumptions adjusted deterministically by demand score. It is an estimate and never a guaranteed result.
