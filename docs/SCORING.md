# Scoring

The standalone engine computes a weighted normalized score:

- Demand 20%
- Competition 14% (higher means more favorable competitive conditions)
- Accessibility 16%
- POI 12%
- Infrastructure 14%
- Flood risk 10% (higher means safer)
- Site area 8%
- Business potential 6%

Thresholds: 90 Excellent, 75 High Potential, 60 Potential, 40 Low Potential, otherwise Not Recommended. High flood risk overrides the band with Requires Investigation. Configuration version demo-v1.0 is included in output. Production configurations need approval, effective dates, immutable versions, validation, and audit.
